Green Wave: Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

In short: Green Wave has no accounts and we, the makers of the game, do not collect any personal data.

The game shows an ad only when a player chooses to watch a short video to keep a run going. Those ads are provided by Google, are never personalised, and do not use the advertising ID.

Who we are

Green Wave ("the game", package name app.greenwave.game) is published by Green Wave ("we"). You can reach us at greenwavelights@gmail.com.

Data we collect

None. The game has no sign-up, no login and no server of ours. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (stars, daily challenge results, endless scores) and your settings are saved on your device only. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads. An ad is shown only when the player taps the button to watch a video and continue a run. There are no banners and no ads between levels.

Because children may play Green Wave, every player is treated as a child for advertising:

  • Ads are never personalised. There is no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.
  • The game does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

To deliver and measure these ads, Google's advertising software inside the game collects the following automatically and sends it to Google:

DataWhat it is
IP addressMay be used to estimate general location (for example, country or city).
Device identifiersThe app set ID, which is not an advertising ID and cannot be used to follow a player across other developers' apps.
App interactionsApp launches, taps and video views related to ads.
DiagnosticsPerformance information such as crash logs and launch time.

Google uses this data for serving ads, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit. Google's use of the data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads are optional. If the video button is never tapped, no ad is shown.

Sharing and rating

If a grown-up taps Share and passes the check, the game opens Android's share sheet with a short text about your result. You choose the app and the recipient; nothing is sent to us. If you are asked to rate the game, the rating card is provided by Google Play.

Children

Green Wave is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect personal data from children or from anyone else. The only data that leaves the device is the advertising data described above, collected by Google under its child-directed settings.

Sharing a result opens another app, so the game first asks a grown-up to answer a simple sum.

Parents and guardians with questions can write to greenwavelights@gmail.com.

Deleting your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove the data saved on your device, uninstall the game. For data held by Google, use the controls described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: greenwavelights@gmail.com.
