Green Wave: Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

In short: Green Wave has no accounts and never asks for a real name, email address or contacts. The only thing a player can choose to share is a made-up nickname for the optional daily leaderboard.

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional videos to keep a run going, and a short full-screen ad between runs. They are never personalised and do not use the advertising ID.

Who we are

Green Wave ("the game", package name app.greenwave.game) is published by Green Wave ("we"). You can reach us at greenwavelights@gmail.com.

Data we collect

Only if a player uses the optional daily leaderboard (see "Leaderboard" below): a made-up nickname, the daily results the player posts, an optional country flag, and a random anonymous ID. Nothing is collected if the leaderboard is never opened. The game has no sign-up, no login and no password. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (stars, daily challenge results, endless scores) and your settings are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us, except the daily results posted to the leaderboard if the player opens it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show two kinds of ads: rewarded videos, which appear only when the player taps the button to watch one and continue a run, and a short full-screen ad that may appear between runs, after a level or an endless run. Ads never appear during a run or in the daily challenge, and there are no banners.

Because children may play Green Wave, every player is treated as a child for advertising:

  • Ads are never personalised. There is no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.
  • The game does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

To deliver and measure these ads, Google's advertising software inside the game collects the following automatically and sends it to Google:

DataWhat it is
IP addressMay be used to estimate general location (for example, country or city).
Device identifiersThe app set ID, which is not an advertising ID and cannot be used to follow a player across other developers' apps.
App interactionsApp launches, taps and video views related to ads.
DiagnosticsPerformance information such as crash logs and launch time.

Google uses this data for serving ads, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit. Google's use of the data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Rewarded videos are optional: if the video button is never tapped, none is shown.

Leaderboard

The daily leaderboard is optional and is opened by the player. To use it the player picks a nickname. Nicknames must be made up: the game asks players never to use a real name or contact details, rejects nicknames that contain contact details or offensive words, and lets players report a name, which then disappears from the reporter's boards and is reviewed by us.

If the player uses the leaderboard, the following is stored online with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), where other players can see the nickname, flag and results:

DataWhat it is
NicknameChosen by the player, 3 to 16 characters.
Country flagOptional and off by default. Only a two-letter country code taken from the phone's language setting is stored, never a location.
Daily resultsThe average wait of each daily challenge the player posts, and the date.
Anonymous IDA random ID created by Firebase when the leaderboard is first opened. It is not linked to an email, a name or the advertising ID.
Friends group codeA six-character code, if the player creates or joins a friends group. Players in a group can see each other's nicknames and results.

Google, as Firebase's provider, also receives the device's IP address when the game contacts it. See the Firebase privacy information. We use this data only to show the leaderboards and to keep them fair. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing and rating

If a grown-up taps Share and passes the check, the game opens Android's share sheet with a short text about your result. You choose the app and the recipient; nothing is sent to us. If you are asked to rate the game, the rating card is provided by Google Play.

Children

Green Wave is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect personal data from children or from anyone else. The data that can leave the device is the advertising data described above, collected by Google under its child-directed settings, and, only if a child opens the optional leaderboard, a made-up nickname and daily results. The leaderboard has no free chat and no messages: other players see only a nickname, a flag and a time.

Sharing a result opens another app, so the game first asks a grown-up to answer a simple sum.

Parents and guardians with questions can write to greenwavelights@gmail.com.

Deleting your data

To remove the data saved on your device, uninstall the game. To have your leaderboard entries removed, write to greenwavelights@gmail.com with your nickname and roughly when you played, and we will delete them. For data held by Google, use the controls described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
